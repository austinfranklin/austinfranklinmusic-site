The Freesound Player is a digital instrument developed in Cycling '74 Max that uses the Freesound API to make requests for as many as 16 sound samples filtered based on sound content from the online repository [Freesound.org](https://freesound.org). Once returned, the samples are loaded into buffers and can be performed using a MIDI controller and processed in a variety of ways.

*Not available for download.*
