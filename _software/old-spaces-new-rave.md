This application delivers a live binaural audio stream, rendered directly from a web server to a listener's phone in real time. Sound sources are anchored to physical coordinates in the forest, using the new MPEG-I immersive audio standard to stream location-based merged reality audio over a standard mobile web browser. This showcase was the culmination of the IRESAP research project, funded by KK-stiftelsen, developed alongside Mälardalens universitet, the Royal College of Music in Stockholm, Ericsson, Spotify, and teenage engineering.
